DRAFT CLINICAL STUDY PROTOCOL - SYNOPSIS
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. NOT A REAL STUDY.

Protocol title: A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Once-Weekly SYN-4471 Added to Metformin in Adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Protocol number: SYN-T2D-301
Version: Draft 0.3
Indication: Type 2 diabetes mellitus
Phase: 3

1. BACKGROUND AND RATIONALE
SYN-4471 is a fictional once-weekly injectable incretin co-agonist. Phase 2 data (synthetic) showed a dose-dependent reduction in HbA1c over 26 weeks. This study is intended to confirm glycaemic efficacy and safety when SYN-4471 is added to stable metformin therapy.

2. OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS
Primary objective: to demonstrate superiority of SYN-4471 over placebo in change from baseline in HbA1c at Week 52.
Key secondary endpoints: change in body weight, proportion of participants reaching HbA1c below 7.0%, change in fasting plasma glucose.
Safety endpoints: adverse events, hypoglycaemia episodes, laboratory parameters.

3. STUDY DESIGN
Multicentre, randomized (1:1), double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study with a 2-week screening period, a 52-week treatment period and a 4-week safety follow-up.

Study population: Middle-aged adults with type 2 diabetes inadequately controlled on metformin alone
Planned enrollment: Approximately 900 participants will be randomized.
Planned countries and sites: The study will be conducted at 8 sites in 2 countries: United States (5 sites) and China (3 sites).
Planned recruitment period: 12 months

4. INCLUSION CRITERIA
1. Male or female participants aged 40 to 60 years at screening.
2. Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus for at least 5 years.
3. HbA1c between 7.5% and 8.5% at screening.
4. BMI between 27 and 32 kg/m2.
5. On a stable dose of metformin of at least 1500 mg per day for at least 6 months before screening.
6. eGFR of at least 90 mL/min/1.73m2.
7. Fasting C-peptide above the lower limit of normal.
8. Willing and able to perform self-monitoring of blood glucose four times daily and to keep an electronic diary.
9. Able to provide written informed consent.

5. EXCLUSION CRITERIA
1. Type 1 diabetes or secondary forms of diabetes.
2. Any use of insulin, other than short-term use during hospitalisation, within 12 months before screening.
3. Treatment with any glucose-lowering medicine other than metformin within 6 months before screening.
4. History of myocardial infarction, stroke, unstable angina or heart failure at any time.
5. Uncontrolled hypertension, defined as systolic blood pressure above 140 mmHg at screening.
6. ALT or AST more than 1.5 times the upper limit of normal.
7. History of pancreatitis or gallbladder disease.
8. History of any malignancy at any time.
9. Diabetic retinopathy of any grade.
10. Current smokers, or former smokers who stopped less than 5 years ago.
11. Alcohol consumption of more than 7 units per week.
12. Pregnant or breastfeeding women, or women of childbearing potential not using highly effective contraception.
13. Any psychiatric disorder, including treated depression.
14. Participation in another clinical trial within 12 months before screening.
15. Night-shift workers or participants planning travel across more than two time zones during the study.

6. STUDY TREATMENT
SYN-4471 or matching placebo administered subcutaneously once weekly for 52 weeks, added to background metformin.

7. STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS
A sample of 900 randomized participants gives 90% power to detect a treatment difference of 0.4% in HbA1c, assuming a standard deviation of 1.1% and 15% dropout.
