DRAFT CLINICAL STUDY PROTOCOL - SYNOPSIS
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY. NOT A REAL STUDY.

Protocol title: A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Active-Controlled Study of Inhaled SYN-2205/Formoterol Compared With Budesonide/Formoterol in Adolescents and Adults with Moderate to Severe Asthma
Protocol number: SYN-AST-302
Version: Draft 0.2
Indication: Asthma
Phase: 3

1. BACKGROUND AND RATIONALE
SYN-2205 is a fictional inhaled corticosteroid designed for once-daily dosing. This study compares a fixed-dose combination of SYN-2205 and formoterol with an established twice-daily combination.

2. OBJECTIVES AND ENDPOINTS
Primary objective: to show non-inferiority of SYN-2205/formoterol to budesonide/formoterol in change from baseline in trough FEV1 at Week 24.
Key secondary endpoints: annualised rate of severe exacerbations, Asthma Control Questionnaire score, rescue medication use.
Safety endpoints: adverse events, oral candidiasis, morning serum cortisol.

3. STUDY DESIGN
Multicentre, randomized (1:1), double-blind, double-dummy, parallel-group study with a 4-week run-in period, a 24-week treatment period and a 2-week follow-up.

Study population: Adolescents and adults with asthma not adequately controlled on medium-dose inhaled corticosteroids
Planned enrollment: Approximately 640 participants will be randomized.
Planned countries and sites: The study will be conducted at 95 sites in 6 countries: United States (30 sites), Germany (15 sites), Poland (15 sites), Spain (12 sites), Japan (13 sites) and Argentina (10 sites).
Planned recruitment period: 14 months

4. INCLUSION CRITERIA
- Participants aged 12 years or older at screening.
- Physician-diagnosed asthma for at least 12 months.
- Pre-bronchodilator FEV1 between 40% and 90% of predicted normal.
- Treated with a medium or high dose of inhaled corticosteroid, with or without a long-acting beta-agonist, for at least 3 months.
- At least one asthma exacerbation requiring systemic corticosteroids in the previous 12 months.
- Able to provide written informed consent, or assent with consent of a parent or guardian.

5. EXCLUSION CRITERIA
- Current smokers or former smokers with a smoking history of more than 10 pack-years.
- Diagnosis of COPD or another clinically significant lung disease.
- Respiratory infection within 4 weeks before screening.
- Treatment with a biologic for asthma within 6 months before screening.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
- Known hypersensitivity to any component of the study treatments.

6. STUDY TREATMENT
SYN-2205/formoterol once daily or budesonide/formoterol twice daily by dry-powder inhaler for 24 weeks, with matching placebo inhalers to maintain the blind.

7. STATISTICAL CONSIDERATIONS
A sample of 640 randomized participants gives 90% power to show non-inferiority with a margin of 50 mL in trough FEV1, assuming a standard deviation of 180 mL and 10% dropout.
